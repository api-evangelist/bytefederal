# Byte Federal

> Byte Federal is America's leading Bitcoin ATM network operator with over 1,350 locations nationwide, providing secure cryptocurrency buying services through physical ATM machines. The company also offers complementary fintech solutions including ByteConnect point-of-sale systems, ByteWallet mobile app, and Vault custody services.

Byte Federal's mission is to utilize cryptocurrency technology to restore economic power and prosperity to people through sound money principles. Founded on the belief that Bitcoin and blockchain technology can provide an alternative to traditional fractional reserve banking, the company operates the largest network of Bitcoin ATMs in the United States.

Key products and services:
- **Bitcoin ATMs**: Over 1,350 locations for buying Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies with cash
- **ByteConnect**: Point-of-sale system allowing merchants to accept cryptocurrency payments
- **ByteWallet**: Mobile cryptocurrency wallet with Lightning Network support
- **Vault**: Secure cryptocurrency custody and storage solutions
- **ByteU**: Educational platform for cryptocurrency learning
- **ByteVerify**: Pay-per-call live face-to-government-ID match for software and AI agents, paid with x402; inputs deleted after the check

The company serves over 150,000+ customers worldwide and maintains a strong focus on regulatory compliance, user education, and expanding cryptocurrency adoption.
